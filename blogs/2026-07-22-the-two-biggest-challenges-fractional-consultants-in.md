Fractional consulting is one of the fastest-growing engagement models in business today, and the hospitality industry is no exception. Hotel groups, restaurant operators, and hospitality management companies increasingly recognise that they don’t always need a full-time hire. They need senior-level expertise, deployed flexibly, at the right moment.
